FAQs

What is Scratch used for?
Scratch is used by kids, students, and beginners to learn coding in a fun and interactive way.
Is Scratch hard to learn?
Not at all! Scratch is designed for kids and beginners with a simple drag-and-drop interface.
Can I submit a comment without logging in?
Yes. Anyone can leave a comment, you do not need an account.
